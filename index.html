ChessLoop Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains how the ChessLoop mobile app ("ChessLoop", "the app") for Android and iOS handles your information. ChessLoop is published by NoReality ("we", "us").

In short: ChessLoop has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your courses and training progress stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what's needed to process an optional in-app purchase and to deliver app updates.

Information stored on your device

ChessLoop stores the following data locally, using your device's app storage:

  • Your repertoire choices and setup answers (for example, experience level and preferred style)
  • Training progress, review schedules, and scores
  • App settings (for example, sound, vibration, and board theme)
  • Whether you have unlocked ChessLoop Full, and which free lines you've started
  • Any line reports you create with the in-app flag button

We do not receive this data, and it is never uploaded to our servers. We don't operate any servers that collect user data.

Information shared with third parties

ServiceWhenWhat is processed
Google Play / Apple App Store When you buy or restore ChessLoop Full Your purchase is handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We never see or store your payment details.
RevenueCat (purchase management) When the app checks your purchase status, shows the price, or processes a purchase or restore A random, anonymous app user ID generated by the app, purchase and transaction records, store receipts, and basic device information (such as OS version, app version, country/locale, and IP address). This information is used only to verify and restore your purchase. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
Expo (EAS Update) When the app checks for updates Technical information needed to deliver the correct update, such as platform, app and runtime version, update channel, and IP address. See the Expo Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Line reports you choose to send

If you flag a problem with a course line, the report is saved on your device. It is sent only if you choose to share it with your device's share sheet (for example, by email or a messaging app). It then goes to whoever you choose, through the app you pick. A report contains the course, the moves, the board position, and any note you wrote.

Permissions

ChessLoop plays sound effects. Its audio library may list microphone or media-playback permissions on some devices, but ChessLoop never records audio or accesses your microphone. The app doesn't access your contacts, location, camera, photos, or other personal files.

Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: You can delete it at any time with Settings → Erase all progress in the app, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Purchase records: Google, Apple, and RevenueCat keep transaction records as needed to provide restores and meet legal and accounting requirements. To ask for the RevenueCat record linked to your anonymous ID to be deleted, contact us at the address below.

Children

ChessLoop is a general-audience app and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app has no accounts and stores progress only on the device, it collects no personal information from any user beyond the purchase and update data described above.

Security

Data sent to RevenueCat, Expo, Google, and Apple is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it is processed. Since we hold no personal data on our own systems, most requests can be handled on your device as described above. For anything else, contact us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how ChessLoop handles data, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
